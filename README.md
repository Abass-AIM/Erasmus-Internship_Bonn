## Erasmus+ Research Internship — University of Bonn 🇩🇪

Institute of Agricultural Engineering and Robotics, University of Bonn
Research Area: Soil Sensing, Spectroscopy, Artificial Intelligence & Agricultural Robotics

During my Erasmus+ research internship at the University of Bonn, I worked on the development and integration of spectral sensing and artificial intelligence techniques for soil property determination.

My work focused on integrating optical sensing components into a cone penetrometer to enable rapid and potentially in-field characterization of soil properties. I worked withUV-VIS/NIR spectroscopy, soil sensing, data acquisition, and the integration of sensing technologies for agricultural applications.

Main Areas of Work

* Soil property determination using spectral information
* UV-VIS/NIR spectroscopy** for soil sensing
* Integration of optical components into a cone penetrometer
* Investigation of soil-related parameters including N, P, K, Mg, C, and H₂O
* Spectral data acquisition and preprocessing
* GPS/GISmintegration for spatial soil mapping
* AI and machine-learning approaches for interpreting spectral information
* Agricultural robotics and automated soil sampling
* Experimental work with Ocean Optics NIRQuest spectrometer and integrating-sphere equipment

This internship provided valuable hands-on experience at the intersection of agricultural engineering, spectroscopy, artificial intelligence, sensing technologies, and agricultural robotics, while allowing me to contribute to research aimed at developing more efficient and data-driven approaches to soil assessment.
